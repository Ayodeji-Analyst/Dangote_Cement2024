# Dangote_Cement2024
Commercial &amp; Logistics Intelligence Dashboard for Dangote Cement (2024)

I worked through the full data analytics pipeline — from raw transactional data to actionable business insights:


🔹 Data Cleaning & Preprocessing — handled missing values, formatted date columns, and standardized categorical fields across 500+ transactions
🔹 Exploratory Data Analysis — calculated key statistics including total revenue (₦73.62M), total profit (₦20.12M), fulfillment rate, and regional breakdowns
🔹 Visualizations — built donut charts, bar charts, histograms, and trend lines to uncover patterns across products, regions, and sales reps
🔹 Interactive Dashboard — designed a full Excel dashboard with slicers for Delivery Status, Region, and State — enabling dynamic filtering for stakeholders

📊 Key Insights:
• Bulk Cement leads in revenue across product types
• Only 30% of orders were successfully delivered — a major operational red flag
• North West and South West regions drive the highest sales volume
• Top sales rep (Victoria Roberts) significantly outperforms peers

This project sharpened my skills in Excel, data storytelling, and dashboard design. Always excited to turn raw data into decisions! 💡
